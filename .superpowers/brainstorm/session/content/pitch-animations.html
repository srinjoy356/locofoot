Proposed Logic for Animations

Every event is broken down into an Actor, a Target, and an Action.

  • Goal: Ball flies from Actor to Goal net. "GOAL!" text pops up.
  • Passes: Ball moves from Actor to Target player.
  • Nutmeg: Ball moves from Actor, *through* Defender, back to Actor. Defender shakes.
  • Fouls/Tackles: Actor moves quickly into Target. Collision icon 💥 pops up.
  • Cards: Card icon 🟨/🟥 floats above player. Red card makes player fade and exit.
  • Subs: Player walks off to bench, Sub walks from bench to their spot.
