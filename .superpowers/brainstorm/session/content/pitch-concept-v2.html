Questions for Mobile Design

A

Vertical Pitch (Shown)

Best for mobile scrolling. Attacking is up/down. Leaves room for the timeline underneath without squishing the field.

B

Horizontal Pitch

Traditional left-to-right view, but on mobile it requires either turning the phone sideways or making the players very small.

Character Style

1

Illustrated Avatars

Little 2D characters standing on the pitch (like the mockup), giving it a retro arcade feel.

2

Top-Down Jerseys

Top-down tactical view of circular jerseys with numbers (like a tactical board).
